## What Stampies is

Stampies is a digital loyalty card for small businesses. A customer adds a business card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, earns stamps at the counter, and redeems a reward when the card is full.

## No login, no ads

No login is needed to use Stampies, and no ads are shown.

## Rules for customers

One stamp per visit, one visit per day per business. Stamps cannot be sold or transferred. A reward is claimed at the counter, and the business decides if a reward has expired.

## Rules for businesses

A business sets its own reward, stamp target, and links. Links and lists are made by the business, and they can be cleared. A business must not use Stampies to mislead customers about a reward, and must not add stamps for visits that did not happen.

## Fraud controls

Staff stamps need a 4 digit staff code. A stamp is blocked during the 60 second cooldown, and after one stamp per day. Stampies may refuse a stamp that breaks these rules.

## Availability

Stampies may change, pause, or stop a feature. Pass rules from Apple and Google apply to Wallet cards, and a pass can be limited or rejected by Apple or Google.
