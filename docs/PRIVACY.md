Last updated: 2026-11-20.

Stampy is provided by Fintex Australia Pty Ltd (ABN 60 688 406 108), New South Wales, Australia. Contact: contact@fintex.au. Website: https://fintex.au

## What Stampy collects

Stampy collects as little as possible. A business card holds the business name, the reward, and the stamp count. A business dashboard holds stamp counts and visit counts. No login is needed, and no account is created.

## Location

A Wallet card only knows where the business is. The phone works out when the customer is nearby, on the device. Stampy does not track a customer live, and it does not store a live location history.

## Data on the device

For now, data is stored on the device. Server sync ships with the Wallet pass work. A business can clear its demo data from the Analytics screen.

## Birthday

A birthday is used only to send a birthday treat. It is not shared with other businesses, and it is not sold.

## Ads

No ads are shown in Stampy.
